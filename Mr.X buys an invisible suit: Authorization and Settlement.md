## A Day in the Life of Mr. X

Mr. X visits a sci-fi shop to buy a suit that helps him become invisible at any point. He inserts his _credit_ card at the _merchant's_ _Point-of-Sale (POS)_ device, _types_ his PIN, the transaction succeeds within seconds, and Mr. X leaves for his journey to become invisible. He forgets about the few seconds spent during the transaction and throws away the slip that he received from the merchant. He doesn't bother to remember or he doesn't need to remember what happened in those few seconds. A lot many engineers, architects, system designers, and other brilliant minds have come together to make this a forgettable experience (in a good way) for Mr. X. A fogettable transaction experience is a hallmark of a smooth transaction that didn't create any hassle for the customer or the merchant.
